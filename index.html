Independent reproduction

Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI

A from-scratch reimplementation of Korinek, Jones, Sacher, Cotter and McCrory (2026), Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI, The Anthropic Institute Working Paper No. 2026-02. Written entirely from the equations printed in the paper: no replication package was published with it.

What reproduces

Published tableResult
Table 3, the three scenarios in 2030 (p. 31) 20 rows x 3 scenarios, reproduced to the printed precision, except one cell
Table 5, elasticities of capital supply (p. 37) 5 rows x 8 columns, all cells, including the pegged-rental case

The one disagreement, and the four readings the paper leaves implicit, are set out in the repository README. A coverage note tracks which parts of the paper are implemented and which are not.

The paper itself is not hosted here. It is third-party content. Get it from the Anthropic Institute landing page, which also carries the authors' own scenario explorer. That page called itself Version 1.0 when this reproduction was written, so later versions may move the page and equation numbers cited throughout.
